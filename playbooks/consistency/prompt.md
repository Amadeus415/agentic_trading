# Consistency

Semantically identical outcomes can carry inconsistent prices across dates, outcomes, or venues.

Verify equivalent rules and settlement sources literally. Account for each leg, fees, depth, and non-atomic execution. Never call a textual match arbitrage.

Estimate p_win as the probability that the purchased outcome settles at $1. Target and invalidation govern early exits, not the definition of p_win. State a realistic horizon through settlement, shared event driver, falsifier, and uncertainty. Refresh every existing position. Submit complete directional beliefs; Python owns fees, sizing, and fills. Exclude contracts that merely predict a stock or crypto price. Preserve failed research as a blocker, never cash evidence.
