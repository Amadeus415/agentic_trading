# External evidence

Codex may use the internet, public filings, market-data pages, APIs, news, research, and other public context to make a paper decision. The fund engine does not privilege a specific provider.

Every retained evidence item needs:

- source name and direct URL;
- UTC observation time and, when available, the source's own timestamp;
- a concise factual claim and summary;
- the instruments it supports;
- enough source content to audit how it affected the decision.

Every quote needs its own source name, direct URL, retrieval/observation time, source-market timestamp, instrument ID, asset class, price, and status. The engine separately limits the age of the retrieval and the underlying source price; Codex is responsible for honestly transcribing both.

Do not store credentials, session tokens, private account data, personal information, or full copyrighted articles. Prefer concise facts and direct links.

Prediction resolution requires a direct source that establishes the contract's terminal outcome. The agent must not infer or guess a `0` or `1` settlement merely because the event appears likely.
